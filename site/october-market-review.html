Market review · October 1 – November 5, 2026

Wall Street’s election rally capped a turbulent October

Stocks surged after the U.S. midterms. Earlier in the period, rising fuel prices lifted inflation, the Federal Reserve held rates steady, and the Bank of England moved higher.

Equities · November 4

The Dow jumps 1,569 points after the midterms

The Dow led a broad U.S. rally on November 4, its strongest session since April 2025. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite also climbed as investors digested the election results.

Economy · October 2

U.S. payrolls add just 29,000 jobs

The initial September report put unemployment at 4.2%. The Bureau of Labor Statistics also lowered its combined July and August payroll estimates by 60,000.

Inflation · October 14

Gasoline drives U.S. inflation to 3.7%

September consumer prices rose 0.6% from August and 3.7% from a year earlier. A 9.1% monthly jump in gasoline accounted for more than half of the monthly increase.

Oil · October 14

Oil supply rebounds as the IEA sees demand shrinking

The IEA reported that September supply climbed to 102.3 million barrels a day. Its October outlook projected fourth-quarter demand would fall by 2.5 million barrels a day from a year earlier.
